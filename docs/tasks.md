 4  
 5  Customers have reported that when they add the same product multiple times to their shopping cart, the total quantity does not update correctly and simply replaces the previous count. 11  ## Task 3: Wishlist Functionality
12  
13  Customers have asked for a way to save products they like for later consideration.
